LAW ENFORCEMENT & PUBLIC SAFETY PLATFORM

AI-Based Missing Person Identification & Tracking System

A centralized investigation-support workbench designed for law enforcement officers and public safety agencies. Combines OpenCV YuNet face detection, SFace deep facial feature representations (128-D normalized vectors), and responsible human-in-the-loop candidate matching to locate missing individuals rapidly and ethically.

IMMEDIATE EMERGENCY ASSISTANCE: If you believe an individual is in imminent danger or immediate medical distress, call Emergency 112 / Police 100 first before filing an online report.
EMERGENCY: 112

Privacy by Design

Public portals provide no browsing of missing persons rosters, raw biometric embeddings, or suspect identity decisions. Strict server-side RBAC ensures sensitive records remain confidential to assigned officers.

Human in Control

AI similarity scores are mathematical ranking signals, not conclusive determinations. Autonomous identification and automated case closure are architecturally forbidden. Every match requires written officer justification.

Biometric Pipeline

Dual-stage computer vision: YuNet DNN locates faces and 5 key landmarks; SFace extracts 128-dimensional L2-normalized embeddings; pairwise cosine distance ranks Top-K candidate leads against active cases.

Implemented Six-Module Architecture

Fulfills the Professional Training I presentation roadmap and PRD requirements.
