How to Navigate & Operate the AI Missing Person Platform

A step-by-step forensic walk-through detailing how each module functions, how to hover for guidance, and how to execute an investigation lifecycle from intake to recovery.

Interactive Hover System Active

Every button, navigation tab, status badge, and control across this workbench contains intelligent contextual hover tooltips. Whenever you hover your mouse cursor over any element, the system displays the corresponding step number, mathematical rationale, and recommended next action.

The 6 Investigation Lifecycle Phases:

PHASE 1 Case Registration & Facial Reference Vectorization

User Action: Click the black + Enroll Case button in the top navigation bar. Enter the missing person's name, age, gender, CCTNS FIR Number, General Diary (GD) entry, Police Station jurisdiction, and upload a clear, unaltered reference passport portrait.
Computer Vision Engine: OpenCV YuNet validates that the reference image contains exactly one frontal human face (≥60×60px) and extracts 5 facial landmark coordinates. SFace then computes a 128-dimensional L2-normalized feature vector and stores it in the local SQLite database.

PHASE 2 Citizen Sighting Submission & Cryptographic Receipt

User Action: Navigate to the Submit Sighting tab. Upload an image of an individual spotted in public (e.g. railway concourse, bus terminal, market), select the exact location on the interactive map or click a quick-location preset, and submit.
Privacy & Receipt: The system computes a SHA-256 cryptographic hash of the submission, generating an anonymous receipt code (e.g. STG-2026-0001). Citizens can track the progress of their lead without exposing any sensitive police case rosters.

PHASE 3 AI Biometric Matching & CLAHE Low-Light Enhancement

Automatic Processing: When a sighting is received, YuNet detects all candidate faces. If the photo has low contrast or dark shadows (common in evening surveillance), the built-in Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization (CLAHE) filter automatically enhances facial edge details.
Metric Distance: SFace extracts 128-D vectors and computes cosine similarity against all open cases: $$\text{Cosine Similarity} = \frac{\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}}{\|\mathbf{u}\| \|\mathbf{v}\|}$$ Any pair with similarity score $\ge 0.363$ (calibrated $\ge 60\%$ match confidence) is flagged and routed directly to the Review Queue.

PHASE 4 Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop Review & Adjudication

Investigator Oversight: Under strict CCTNS ethical guidelines, the AI is mathematically prohibited from confirming identities automatically. The assigned officer opens the Review Queue, examines the reference photo and sighting photo side-by-side with facial landmark HUDs, and selects one of four decisions:
• Verified Match: Confirms positive identification; updates case status to High Lead.
• Reject Lead (False Positive): Discards mismatch to eliminate false investigative trails.
• Request More Info: Demands higher resolution evidence or field witness interview.
• Escalate to Field Team: Dispatches patrol units to the sighting coordinates.
Every adjudication requires a recorded rationale chip and officer badge signature, permanently logged to the SHA-256 hash-chained audit trail.

PHASE 5 Real-Time CCTV Surveillance Watchlist & Alert Dispatch

Live Stream Ingestion: Connect RTSP camera streams or upload forensic surveillance video files. The engine samples video frames at 5 FPS, detects multiple faces simultaneously, and compares each face against the active missing watchlist in under 15ms per frame.
Priority Alerts: Upon detection, auditory alerts chime and visual priority banners display the camera location, match score, and video timestamp. Officers can click Dispatch Unit, Acknowledge, or Dismiss.

PHASE 6 GIS Corridor Tracking, Forensic Dossier Export & Recovery

Geospatial Trajectory: The GIS Map tab displays chronological sighting clusters across Mappls (MapmyIndia) or OpenStreetMap layers, visualizing the subject's transit corridor from the point of disappearance.
Judicial Documentation: In the Cases Dossier tab, officers can export tamper-evident, watermarked Biometric Forensic Examination Certificates and print AMBER Alert posters with QR verification codes. When the individual is safely recovered, the case status is committed as RESOLVED with official closing notes.
